Ordering Information

Group Dishes & Advance Notice

Large family dishes (like Mashwi Jidr and Group Machboos) require a minimum of 3 hours advance notice for preparation. These can be pre-ordered on our platform.

Operating Hours

Breakfast is served from 8:00 AM to 12:30 PM. The main menu is available from 1:00 PM to 11:00 PM. (Note: Abu Fatira branch serves the main menu only).

Third-Party Platforms

You can also find Dar Hamad on major delivery platforms in Kuwait, including Talabat and Deliveroo, though the full menu and special offers are best accessed via our official site.
