Our Heritage

Where Memory
Becomes Flavour

The original Dar Hamad stood directly on the sandy shores of the Arabian Gulf — a family home where Al-Hamad family welcomed guests through Kuwait's most radiant years, from the 1950s through the 1980s.

Today, the restaurant carries that same spirit of hospitality forward. Every detail — the perforated mashrabiya screens, the azure-blue shuttered windows, the ornate wooden doors with brass knockers — is a conversation between past and present.

The kitchen draws from the Arabian Peninsula, Persia, India, and the Mediterranean: the very trade routes that shaped the Kuwaiti palate across centuries.
